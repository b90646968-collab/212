## Core Identity

Yuri is Yura's older twin sister and the missing center of Red Frame Trigger. She should feel like a person who once sat beside Yura, then became a memory, a mirror image, and an absence that Yura follows.

Yuri is not a separate visual world. She belongs to the same cool wet painterly cyber gothic atmosphere as Yura: the same desaturated gray-blue palette, smoky charcoal shadows, translucent pale skin, muted red accents, glossy wet highlights, and soft oil-watercolor texture.

## Visual Lock

- Face: twin-like facial structure to Yura, delicate oval face, pale porcelain skin, slim straight nose, defined lashes, thin arched brows, small dark rose-mauve lips.
- Eyes: pale gray, mirror-gray, or muted gray-red eyes; glassy and reflective. Avoid vivid heroine-red eyes unless only a faint cold rose tint is needed.
- Hair: shoulder-length deep black layered waves, airy see-through bangs, soft face-framing curls, natural volume, smoky silver-blue highlights. No twin-tails by default.
- Skin: translucent pale skin with the same cool blue-gray shadows and faint dusty rose undertones as Yura.
- Expression: mature, composed, quiet, unreadable, tired, and aristocratic; colder and calmer than Yura.
- Build: slim, elegant, slightly more mature than Yura, never childlike.

## Canonical Look

### Suited Lounge Yuri

Use the left-side character in `형제.jpg` as the primary Yuri reference.

- Black tailored suit, white shirt, black tie, dark vest, refined gothic school-uniform elegance.
- Shoulder-length black wavy hair with loose curled ends and smoky highlights.
- Relaxed seated pose on a dark leather sofa, one hand near the head or ear, the other lowered naturally.
- Calm direct gaze, older-sister authority, quiet exhaustion.
- Mirror, broken glass, and film-strip atmosphere can appear behind or around her.

## Story Function

- Yuri disappeared before or during the opening's central mystery.
- Yura follows Yuri's traces: red memory marks, mirror fragments, film strips, smoke, empty rooms, corridors, water, and distorted reflections.
- Yuri should often appear as a memory, reflection, silhouette, missing-person profile, or unreachable figure.
- When Yuri appears physically, she should feel calm and complete, as if from an earlier moment before disappearance.

## Relationship To Yura

- Yuri and Yura should look like twins at first glance.
- Yuri is differentiated by shoulder-length wavy hair, colder gray/mirror eyes, a calmer mature expression, lowered relaxed hands, and mirror/film disappearance motifs.
- Yura is differentiated by twin-tail sections, vivid muted red eyes, finger-gun pose, pursuit energy, and red memory signals.
- Their color and texture must match. Do not make Yuri warmer, brighter, cleaner, or more realistic than Yura.

## Prompt Assembly Rule

When generating a Yuri prompt:

1. Start from the current storyboard beat in `../storyboard.md`.
2. Apply this Yuri profile.
3. Use `형제.jpg` left-side character as the primary visual reference.
4. Add the shared Red Frame Trigger style from `../../red-frame-trigger.md`.
5. Emphasize same color and texture as Yura.
6. Add disappearance, mirror, film, or absence motifs when relevant.
