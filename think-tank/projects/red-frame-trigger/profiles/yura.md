## Core Identity

Yura is the main heroine of Red Frame Trigger: a fragile but dangerous girl with a quiet, direct gaze. She should feel like the emotional center of the opening, caught between grief, memory, and controlled threat.

## Visual Lock

- Face: delicate oval face, pale porcelain skin, slim straight nose, small glossy dark rose-mauve lips, long lashes, sharp but exhausted eyes.
- Eyes: vivid muted red or deep wine-red eyes; tired, glassy, slightly haunted, never bright cheerful red.
- Hair: deep blue-black hair with straight full bangs. Default hair shape is twin-tail or twin-tail sections, with loose painterly strands and soft blue highlights.
- Skin: translucent pale skin with cool blue-gray shadows and faint dusty rose undertones.
- Expression: quiet, intense, numb, melancholic, and dangerous; avoid open-mouth shock, cute smiles, or exaggerated anger.
- Build: slim, delicate, elegant, not childlike.

## Canonical Looks

### Suited Lounge Yura

Use the right-side character in `형제.jpg` as the suited Yura reference.

- Black tailored suit, white shirt, black tie, dark vest, refined gothic school-uniform feeling.
- Twin-tail sections, straight bangs, red eyes, pale skin.
- Lounging on a dark leather sofa, one arm relaxed, one hand near the mouth with cigarette smoke.
- Mood: controlled, aloof, exhausted, intimate, quietly dangerous.

### Nightmare Close-Up Yura

Use `유라 악몽최종.png` as the nightmare/psychological close-up reference.

- Face fills the frame from above or near-frontal.
- Wet, heavy bangs over the forehead; dark twin-tail hair spreading into the background.
- Red eyes half-open, exhausted and dissociated.
- Pale skin with droplets or tear-like highlights.
- Mood: waking from nightmare, numb fear, silent grief.

### Signature Finger-Gun Yura

Use `유라 반지제거.png` as the signature pose reference.

- Yura faces forward with direct eye contact.
- One hand makes a finger-gun toward the camera.
- The other open palm supports the gesture from below, close to the foreground.
- No rings by default. A bracelet or simple wrist detail can exist only when it matches the reference or is requested.
- Hands must be anatomically careful: five fingers per hand, no extra fingers, no fused fingers, no warped palms.

## Story Motifs

- Red memory traces, red thread, reflected red light, eye reflections.
- Smoke, water, mirror fragments, film fragments, empty rooms, wet hair, pale skin, cold night light.
- Yura should often be framed by absence: empty space, missing Yuri, distant rooms, corridors, water, or a black void.
