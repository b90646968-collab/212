# Runway Video Prompt

## Main Video Prompt

```text
A cinematic fashion runway video featuring an original fictional female character walking confidently down a luxury runway for the fictional brand AURELIA NOIR.

Scene:
A dark glossy runway with mirror-like reflections, silver spotlights, subtle fog, camera flashes, elegant audience silhouettes, and a massive abstract metallic brand installation in the background.

Character:
Original fictional female fashion model with futuristic K-pop idol energy, long glossy black hair, refined makeup, confident expression, and graceful runway posture.
She wears a black-and-silver sculptural haute couture gown with metallic shoulder details, crystal accents, and futuristic heels.

Action:
She walks slowly and confidently toward the camera, turns at the end of the runway, poses with one hand near her waist, then looks directly into the camera.
The dress moves naturally with each step. Hair and fabric have realistic motion.

Camera:
Low-angle tracking shot, smooth dolly movement, cinematic close-up, luxury fashion week atmosphere.

Lighting:
High-contrast silver and white spotlights, glossy reflections, premium editorial mood.

Style:
Photorealistic, cinematic, luxury fashion film, 4K, high detail, elegant, dramatic, premium runway campaign.
Do not imitate any real person. Do not use real brand logos.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
real celebrity likeness, copied face, existing character, real brand logo, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed body, bad anatomy, low quality, blurry, plastic skin, unrealistic walking, glitchy motion, text artifacts, watermark, cheap costume, cosplay look
```
