# Fictional Brand Prompt

## Brand Concept

```text
Design a fictional luxury fashion brand for this character.
Brand name: AURELIA NOIR.
Brand identity: futuristic elegance, dark glamour, metallic couture, premium K-pop runway energy.
Apply the brand to the character's outfit, accessories, stage design, and visual campaign.

Create a full runway look:
A black chrome couture gown, silver sculptural shoulder pieces, subtle monogram pattern, crystal hair accessory, futuristic earrings, and glossy black platform heels.
The design should feel like a luxury fashion week collection, not cosplay.
```

## Brand Name Ideas

```text
1. AURELIA NOIR - dark luxury, silver couture
2. VELORA - elegant futuristic femininity
3. NYXELLE - cyber runway, mysterious glamour
4. LUMERIA - luminous beauty, premium idol fashion
5. ORVIA - minimal luxury, sculptural silhouettes
```
