Title: Adult Yura Looking Up At A Red Crescent Moon
Aspect ratio: 16:9
Project: Red Frame Trigger Opening

Prompt:
Create a 16:9 anime opening key visual of adult Yura standing alone and looking up at a red crescent moon.

Scene:
Adult Yura stands alone at night, looking up at the same enormous red crescent moon from her childhood memory. She is seen mostly from behind with a subtle side profile, small against a vast dark sky. The space beside her is empty, clearly implying Yuri's absence without showing Yuri physically. The mood is quiet, lonely, and ominous, like the present-day echo of a childhood promise after separation.

Yura identity:
Adult Yura, the main heroine, pale translucent skin, dark blue-black hair with straight bangs and soft twin-tail sections or long side strands moving in the wind, vivid red eyes visible in a faint side profile, delicate facial features, fragile but dangerous presence, quiet intense expression. She wears a dark simple gothic outfit with subtle black ribbons and silver details, elegant and restrained.

Pose:
Yura stands still, head tilted upward toward the red crescent moon. Her arms rest naturally at her sides or one hand lightly touches the edge of her sleeve, no reaching hand, no finger-gun pose, no dramatic action. Her posture feels frozen by memory, not heroic.

Symbolic absence:
Where Yuri once stood, show only a faint empty gap in the composition, with drifting silver-gray dust, tiny mirror glints, torn film-strip fragments, and soft black hair-like wisps disappearing into the wind. Yuri must not appear as a body, ghost, reflection, or visible person.

Environment:
A dark open place at night, like an empty rooftop, hill, or abandoned schoolyard. Cold blue-gray shadows, faint wind, painterly clouds, broken film fragments in the air. The red crescent moon casts a subtle blood-red rim light along Yura's hair, shoulders, and cheek.

Composition:
Cinematic 16:9 horizontal frame. The red crescent moon dominates the upper sky. Adult Yura is placed in the lower third, alone, with large atmospheric negative space and an intentionally empty area beside her. The image should feel like an anime opening flashback match cut from the childhood scene to the present.

Mood:
Melancholic present-day memory, lonely, restrained psychological fantasy, beautiful but unsettling, the feeling of someone looking at the sky after losing her twin.

Art style:
Semi-realistic painterly anime illustration, loose wet digital watercolor brushwork, soft translucent skin rendering, visible brush strokes, slightly unfinished edges, dreamy and melancholic mood, delicate facial features, glossy eyes, subtle wet highlights, cool blue-gray color palette with selective red accents, muted shadows, soft diffused moonlight, elegant cyber gothic atmosphere, cinematic anime opening character design, refined but raw painterly texture, atmospheric negative space.

Avoid:
Yuri physically appearing, child Yura, extra people, ghost body, monster, gore, screaming, hand reaching toward camera, finger-gun pose, distorted hands, extra fingers, fanservice, exposed clothing, bright cheerful colors, flat cel shading, photorealistic 3D, text, logo, watermark.
